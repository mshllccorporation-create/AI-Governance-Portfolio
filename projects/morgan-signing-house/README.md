# Morgan Signing House

Morgan Signing House is the professional practice context for selected work in AI governance, assurance, adoption, business transformation, and operational safety.

This repository uses only public-facing, sanitized descriptions. It does not disclose private intellectual property, proprietary implementation details, confidential client information, agreements, credentials, personal data, or unpublished work.

## Public portfolio themes

- Practical AI governance for organizations adopting emerging systems
- AI readiness, strategic planning, and transformation
- Explainability, assurance, and evidence-based oversight
- AI safety, security, and operational resilience
- Supply-chain, vendor, workflow, and dependency risk
- Research and implementation artifacts that translate governance into practice

For current public information, visit [morgansigninghouse.com](https://www.morgansigninghouse.com).

## Publication standard

Case studies should identify the public source, author role, problem, method, result, limitations, and practical lesson. They should not reproduce private product architecture, confidential assessments, customer material, or restricted academic or sponsor content.
