# Evidence First AI Governance and SOC 2 Readiness

## Academic context

Independent academic assignment completed as part of postgraduate study in AI for business. Grade: 65.

## Problem

Organizations deploying AI often have policies and controls but struggle to show whether those controls operated, whether evidence is complete, and whether risks were corrected before an audit or incident.

## Approach

The assignment proposed an evidence-first governance capability that connects AI governance expectations with SOC 2 control areas, assessment questions, evidence requirements, control gaps, remediation ownership, and continuous review.

## Use cases

- SOC 2 readiness assessment
- AI control and evidence-gap identification
- Continuous compliance monitoring
- Explainability and accountability review
- Risk-based remediation tracking
- Governance reporting for leadership

## Practical lesson

Effective AI governance must be operational and evidence-based. A policy assertion is weaker than a traceable record showing the control, owner, evidence, exception, corrective action, and verification.

## Limitations

This is an academic governance design and not a SOC 2 audit opinion, certification, legal determination, or production assurance result.
