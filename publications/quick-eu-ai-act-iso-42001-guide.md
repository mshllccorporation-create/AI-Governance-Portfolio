# Quick EU AI Act & ISO/IEC 42001 Implementation Guide

**Author:** Horatio Morgan  
**Publisher:** Morgan Signing House  
**Format:** Practical implementation guide  
**Public listing:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GPR97YSS)

## Description

This guide presents a practical way to think about implementation readiness under the EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001. It is designed to help organizations connect governance principles to operational activities such as AI-system inventory, roles and responsibilities, risk assessment, documentation, human oversight, monitoring, evidence, and continual improvement.

## Portfolio relevance

The guide complements the portfolio’s broader work on:

- AI adoption and readiness;
- evidence-led governance and control mapping;
- explainability and accountability;
- operational AI governance;
- supplier and lifecycle governance; and
- assessment, remediation, and verification.

It also demonstrates the ability to translate complex regulatory and standards material into accessible implementation guidance for practitioners.
