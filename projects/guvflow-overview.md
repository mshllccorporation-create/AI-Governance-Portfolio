# GuvFlow governance workflow

**Context:** GuvFlow is an AI governance product associated with Horatio Morgan's architecture and auditing work. This overview is deliberately limited to the public product concept.

## Use case

An organization needs to move from assertions of AI governance to traceable decisions. A practical workflow records the AI use case, accountable owner, applicable obligations, control expectations, evidence, findings, human review, remediation, and follow-up.

## Public demonstration narrative

1. Define the system and decision scope.
2. Record relevant governance questions and evidence references.
3. Identify gaps and uncertainty without presenting missing evidence as proof of compliance.
4. Route material findings to a named reviewer and owner.
5. Track remediation and preserve the decision trail.

The approach is designed to work across model, cloud, evidence, and GRC providers through neutral controls and portable records. This page does not claim a specific live feature, customer result, or certification. Product details are available at [GuvFlow](https://www.guvflow.com/).
