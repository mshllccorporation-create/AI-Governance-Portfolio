# Morgan Signing House | AI-assisted website and governance content

**Public reference:** [morgansigninghouse.com](https://www.morgansigninghouse.com)

## Problem

An AI governance practice needs a public experience that explains complex ideas clearly while demonstrating disciplined information architecture, evidence-oriented thinking, and usable pathways for services, research, training, and client intake.

## What I built

I designed and maintain the Morgan Signing House website using AI-assisted development. The public site brings together governance framework content, service descriptions, research and insights, training and Academy access, downloads, and intake pathways.

## Design decisions

- Organize governance material around understandable operational layers.
- Separate educational content from service and intake pathways.
- Make research and supporting resources discoverable.
- Present governance as a process of evidence, ownership, escalation, and accountability rather than as a static policy library.
- Use AI-assisted development while retaining human review of structure, content, and public claims.

## Work sample

The public website is the artifact. This repository documents the design rationale and governance lessons without copying private implementation details or unpublished materials.
