# AI GEA SME Cohort

## Public portfolio context

The AI Governance and Explainability Assessment SME cohort was designed to help small and medium-sized enterprises understand their AI governance baseline, explainability needs, maturity, risks, and practical improvement opportunities.

## Use case

The cohort model provides a structured pathway for organizations that use or plan to use AI in areas such as analytics, customer service, fraud detection, operational planning, and decision support.

## Assessment activities

- Baseline review of AI use and governance practices
- Governance maturity assessment
- Review of gaps and operational risks
- Guided explainability and oversight assessment
- Structured participant feedback on clarity and usability
- Practical improvement planning

## Practical value

The use case demonstrates how AI governance support can be made accessible to SMEs through a focused assessment, clear maturity feedback, evidence-based blind-spot identification, and practical next steps rather than an enterprise-scale compliance burden.
