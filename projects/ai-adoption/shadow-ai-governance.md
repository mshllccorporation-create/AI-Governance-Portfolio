# Shadow AI Governance

Shadow AI governance should identify unauthorized AI use, assign ownership, assess risk, document decisions, and track remediation through closure.

This is a high-level governance use case. It does not disclose proprietary product architecture, implementation details, confidential assessments, or internal control logic.

## Governance questions

- What AI tool, model, agent, or integration was discovered?
- Who owns the use, business purpose, data, and outcome?
- What systems, data, permissions, and third parties are involved?
- What risks require assessment or treatment?
- What decision was made, by whom, and on what evidence?
- What remediation, restriction, approval, or removal is required?
- How will completion and residual risk be verified?

## Evidence expectations

A defensible process should preserve the discovery record, ownership decision, risk assessment, treatment decision, supporting evidence, action owner, due date, closure status, and verification record.
