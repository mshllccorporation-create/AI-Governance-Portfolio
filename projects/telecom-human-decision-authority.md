# Human decision authority in AI assisted telecom operations

**Context:** Postgraduate Diploma in AI for Business, Module 2 written assignment. The source uses an illustrative telecom organization. It should not be read as a documented deployment at a named telecom company.

## Problem

A telecom business considering AI in customer service, CRM, and sales prediction must decide which judgments can be supported by models and which require human authority. Data quality, bias, customer harm, security, and the timing of escalation affect that choice.

## My approach

I examined the decision process across data readiness, model performance, governance, and human intervention. The assignment discussed data labeling and lineage, held-out validation, fairness checks, explanation methods, feedback loops, and monitoring. It also treated false positives and false negatives as business and customer consequences, rather than only model metrics.

I proposed that customer complaints and ambiguous or consequential decisions have a clear path to a person. Oversight needs named owners, defined thresholds, records of model behavior, and periodic review of outcomes. This is a design recommendation from the assignment, not evidence that a production system achieved those results.

## Portfolio takeaway

The case shows how I translate technical evaluation into operational decision rights: what the model may suggest, what it may execute, and who can correct or stop it. A future implementation would need baseline data, validated thresholds, customer outcome measures, and an independent review of effectiveness.
