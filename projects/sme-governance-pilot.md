# SME explainability and governance pilot

**Context:** A cohort-based AI governance and explainability assessment conducted through the NSF TRAILS pilot, with Morgan Signing House and DataedX Group. The Explainability AI Framework was a core framework referenced and applied in the pilot methodology.

## Problem and approach

Resource-limited small and medium-sized organizations often lack a clear inventory of AI use, accountable owners, documented controls, and evidence of operating practice. The pilot applied the Explainability AI Framework through baseline assessment, evidence review, questionnaires and interviews, guided explainability assessment, feedback on usability, organization-level reporting, and tailored governance or coaching roadmaps.

The framework connected explainability and governance maturity to practical evidence questions covering inventory, ownership, human oversight, monitoring, traceability, contestability, data boundaries, third-party dependencies, and remediation.
