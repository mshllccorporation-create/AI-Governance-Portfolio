# AI Adoption and Agentic Workflow Planning

## Academic context

Independent academic assignment completed as part of postgraduate study in AI in practice. Grade: 72.

## Problem

Cybersecurity and other operational teams may adopt AI to improve planning, forecasting, workflow efficiency, and service delivery while facing uncertainty about data, culture, security, compliance, and agentic behavior.

## Approach

The assignment compared adoption options, considered build-versus-buy tradeoffs, used scenario planning, identified organizational and technical readiness requirements, and proposed measures for adoption, delivery, risk, and operational performance.

## Use cases

- AI adoption-readiness assessment
- Predictive sprint and resource planning
- Agentic workflow assistance with human oversight
- Scenario-based implementation planning
- Change management and workforce adoption
- KPI design for AI-enabled operations

## Practical lesson

AI adoption should be treated as an operating-model decision. The organization must balance value creation with data readiness, human adoption, security, governance, vendor dependence, and the possibility of failure or disruption.

## Limitations

This is an academic proposal rather than a production implementation. Forecasts, ROI estimates, and technical assumptions require validation before use in a real organization.
