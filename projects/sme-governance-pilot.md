# SME explainability and governance pilot

**Context:** A cohort-based AI governance and explainability assessment involving Morgan Signing House and DataedX Group, with NSF TRAILS support described in project materials.

## Problem and approach

Resource-limited small and medium-sized organizations often lack a clear inventory of AI use, accountable owners, documented controls, and evidence of operating practice. The project description proposes baseline assessment, questionnaires and interviews, guided use of an explainability assessment tool, feedback on usability, and tailored governance roadmaps.
