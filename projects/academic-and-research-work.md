# Academic and Research Work

Selected assignments, research papers, and project summaries may be presented as concise case studies.

Each case study should identify the context, grade where disclosed, problem, method, evidence, conclusion, limitations, and practical application to AI governance.

## Selected case studies

- [Data and model governance for AI-enabled operations](academic-and-research-work/module-2-data-and-model-governance.md)
- [AI adoption and agentic workflow planning](academic-and-research-work/module-3-ai-adoption.md)
- [Evidence-first AI governance and SOC 2 readiness](academic-and-research-work/module-4-evidence-first-governance.md)

Academic work should be described as independent coursework or personal research and not as official university policy, institutional research, or a client deliverable unless that authority is documented.
