# AI Governance Portfolio

This portfolio presents selected public-facing work by Horatio Morgan across AI governance, adoption, assurance, operational safety, and transformation.

The work is connected to Morgan Signing House and [GuvFlow](https://www.guvflow.com/#agentic-governance), an operational AI governance approach for mapping AI systems, workflows, owners, data access, dependencies, risks, evidence, and accountability.

## GuvFlow focus

- Map AI systems, agents, tools, workflows, owners, data, and dependencies
- Identify governance, security, privacy, operational, and legal failure modes
- Connect risks to controls, evidence, owners, remediation, and verification
- Govern agentic AI authority, approvals, execution, monitoring, and revocation
- Support continuous assurance rather than one-time policy review
